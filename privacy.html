Trolley Dash: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Trolley Dash is a browser extension, and a companion website at trolley-dash.co.uk, that help you plan meals and add the ingredients to your online supermarket basket. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use either.

In short

Trolley Dash does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data. Everything it remembers stays on your own device, in your browser. The same applies to the website.

What is stored on your device

So that the extension works between visits, it saves the following in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local):

  • The meals you have picked, your favourites and your weekly plan
  • Recipes you create yourself
  • Your settings, such as which kind of product to prefer
  • "Usual shops" you have saved and a short history of past shops
  • A note of the product last used for each ingredient and what it cost, so the same one can be picked next time

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it. You can remove it at any time by removing the extension or clearing its data in your browser.

The website, and syncing with the extension

The website saves your plan in your browser's local storage on your device. It has no accounts and does not send your plan to a server. If you also use the extension, a small part of it runs on trolley-dash.co.uk only, to copy your meals, favourites, plans, pantry and Extras settings between the website and the extension, so the two stay in step. This copying happens entirely inside your browser; nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. The website itself loads no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

What the extension does on supermarket websites

When you press the button to add your meals to your basket, the extension runs on the supported supermarket website you are using. It searches for each ingredient, reads product names and prices on the page to choose a suitable item, and clicks "Add" as you would. This happens inside your browser. The page content it reads is not stored remotely or sent to us or anyone else.

The extension does not read your supermarket password, payment details or account information, and it does not check out for you.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information, browsing history or analytics
  • We do not use advertising or tracking tools
  • We do not sell or transfer data to third parties
  • We do not load or run code from remote servers

Third parties

Trolley Dash is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tesco, Sainsbury's, Asda, Morrisons, Waitrose or Ocado. Your use of those websites is covered by their own terms and privacy policies.
